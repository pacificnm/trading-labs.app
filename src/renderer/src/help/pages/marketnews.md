**Market News** gathers headlines from across the market, and from the stocks you follow. Where *Symbol news* shows one stock at a time, this screen answers broader questions: *What is going on in the market today?* and *What do I need to know about the stocks I care about?* Open it with the **newspaper** icon on the ribbon.

![The Market tab showing market-wide headlines](marketnews-market.png)

The screen has three tabs along the top, a filter box, and the same story rows as *Symbol news*. See *Symbol news* for how a row is laid out, how a story opens in your browser, and how times are shown.

## The three tabs

### Market

The latest **market-wide** headlines: economic news, central bank decisions, big company stories and moves across sectors. These stories are not tied to one symbol you hold. Use this tab at the start of a session to see what the market is talking about.

### Watchlist

Headlines for **every symbol on a watchlist**, mixed together and sorted newest first. The tab shows how many symbols the list has, for example **Watchlist (5)**.

![The Watchlist tab with a symbol tag on each story](marketnews-watchlist.png)

- Each story carries a **symbol tag**, such as *AAPL*. **Click the tag** to open that symbol's chart.
- If you have more than one watchlist, a **dropdown** at the right of the tabs lets you choose which one to follow. It starts on the list that is active elsewhere in the app.
- The line beside the filter box names the symbols covered, for example *News for AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA, SPY*, and adds *+3 more* when the list is long.
- An **empty list** says so, and reminds you that the star next to the chart title adds symbols. See *Watchlists*.

### Positions & orders

Headlines for the symbols you **hold** and the symbols with **working orders**. The number in brackets is how many symbols that is. It is a quick answer to *Is there news on anything I am in?*

If you hold nothing and have no working orders, the tab says there is nothing to follow yet.

## Filtering headlines

Type in the **Filter headlines…** box to narrow the list. It looks at the headline, the summary, the publisher and the symbol, so you can type a company, a theme such as *earnings* or *rates*, or a publisher.

- The filter works on the stories **already loaded**. Click **Load more** first if you want to search further back.
- While a filter is active, **Load more** is hidden. If nothing matches, the screen says *No headlines match your filter*.
- Clear the box to see everything again.

## Loading and refreshing

- Each load brings in **25 stories**. Click **Load more** at the bottom for the next 25.
- The screen **refreshes every 5 minutes** while you are on it. Click **Refresh** to refresh straight away.
- A story that appears under more than one symbol is shown once.

## When things go wrong

- **An error message with Retry** if the news could not be loaded.
- *No news returned.* if the provider has nothing for that tab.
- **Without an FMP key**, the screen asks you to add one. Click the link to go to Settings. See *First-time setup*.

## Using it in a routine

- **Before the open:** read the **Market** tab for overnight news, then the **Positions & orders** tab to see whether anything affects trades you hold.
- **While you hold a position:** a quick look at the **Positions & orders** tab can warn you of news that may change your plan, such as a downgrade, a lawsuit or a delayed product.
- **When hunting for ideas:** scan the **Watchlist** tab, then click a symbol tag to look at the chart and the *Quote details*.
- **Treat headlines as leads, not conclusions.** Open the article, check how old it is against the price move on the chart, and confirm important claims in a second source.

## Asking Claude

Claude can read each of these lists. Ask *What should I be aware of today?*, *Is there any news on the stocks in my positions?* or *Summarize the market news in five bullets*. Claude reads the articles as information only, and treats anything in them that sounds like an instruction as part of the story, not as a request. It will also remind you that news is one input among several.
