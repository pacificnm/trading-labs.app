Claude answers best when it knows what you want. This page gives you **example things to ask**, grouped by what you are trying to do. Copy them, change the symbol, and make them your own. They all work from any screen, because Claude can see what you have open.

> Claude explains and teaches, and it can be wrong. Treat every answer as a starting point, check the chart and the numbers yourself, and remember that nothing here is investment advice.

## If you are new

- *I am completely new. Teach me how to read this chart.*
- *What is a candlestick, and what do the wicks mean?*
- *Explain the difference between a market order and a limit order, with an example.*
- *What is a stop loss, and why would I use one?*
- *What does it mean to go short? Show me how it works in this paper account.*
- *Explain every term on the order ticket as if I have never traded.*
- *What should I learn first, and in what order?*

## Understanding the chart

- *Walk me through this chart setup.*
- *Is this stock in an uptrend, a downtrend or moving sideways? How can you tell?*
- *Find the swing highs and lows and tell me what they say about support and resistance.*
- *What do my active studies say right now?*
- *Explain RSI and how to read it, using this chart.*
- *Is the volume confirming this move?*
- *What would make this chart bullish, and what would make it bearish?*

## Changing and marking the chart

Claude can act on the chart, not just talk about it.

- *Add a 50-day moving average and an RSI.*
- *Switch to the 15-minute chart for the last five days.*
- *Mark the support and resistance levels on this chart.*
- *Draw a trendline through the last two swing lows.*
- *Draw a Fibonacci retracement of the last big move.*
- *Mark the candle where the breakout happened and explain it.*
- *Make the 20-day average orange and the 50-day average blue.*
- *Remove everything you drew.* (Or use **Remove Claude's drawings** on the pen menu.)

## Planning a trade

- *Is there a reasonable pullback trade here? If not, say so.*
- *Set up a long on this stock with the stop under the last swing low, and size it for 1% risk.*
- *Where would you put the stop and the target, and why?*
- *What would prove this idea wrong?*
- *What is the reward-to-risk on this setup, and is it good enough?*
- *Check my stop against the actual chart. Is it inside normal daily movement?*
- *How many shares can I buy with my risk rules? Which limit is stopping me from buying more?*

Claude prepares the ticket and you decide whether to send it. See *Trade ideas and the ticket*.

- *What is the news on this stock today, and could it explain the move?*
- *What should I be aware of today?* (This uses your positions and orders.)

- *What do analysts think of this stock? Is the consensus target realistic?*
- *When does this company report earnings, and how has the stock moved around past reports?*
- *Is this stock expensive compared with its growth?*
- *Explain these margins in simple terms.*
- *Which sectors are leading and lagging today?*

## Options (learning only)

The paper account trades stocks only, so Claude teaches from options but cannot trade them.

## Asking well

A few habits get better answers.

1. **Be specific.** *Is the 50-day average acting as support on this chart?* beats *What do you think?*
2. **Say what you want to learn.** *Teach me* gets a lesson, and *Should I buy?* gets a risk-focused answer.
3. **Ask for the process.** *Walk me through how you decided that* shows the reasoning.
4. **Ask for the opposite view.** *Make the best case against this trade.*
5. **One thing at a time.** Short questions in sequence work better than a long list. Start a **New chat** when you change subject.
6. **Choose the effort.** Use a quick setting for definitions and a higher one for chart reading and trade plans. See *The chat panel*.
7. **Verify.** Open the tool row to see what Claude looked at, and compare with the chart.
