Everything in Trading Lab follows the **current symbol**: the chart, the quote, news, the order ticket and Claude's context all switch when you change it. There are several ways to change it. Pick whichever is quickest for what you are doing.

## Symbol search

The search box at the top left finds a stock or ETF by ticker or company name.

![Typing in the symbol search box](symbols-search.png)

1. Click the box and start typing, for example `APP` or `apple`.
2. Matching results appear in a list under the box. With a market data key, each row shows the ticker, the company name and the exchange.
3. Choose one with the mouse, or use **↑** and **↓** and press **Enter**. **Esc** closes the list.

The app switches to that symbol and clears the box.

- Results come from FMP as you type, after a short pause, so a brief wait is normal.
- You can always press **Enter** on a ticker you typed yourself. If it is not a real symbol, the chart will tell you the data could not be loaded.
- Without a market data key, search only knows a short built-in list of well-known stocks (shown with a **Go to symbol** row for anything else). Add your key to search every listed company. See *First-time setup*.

## The watchlist dropdown

Next to the search box, the **watchlist** button holds the symbols you follow. Its label shows the name of the active list and how many symbols it has. The star is filled when the current symbol is on any of your lists.

![The watchlist dropdown](symbols-watchlist.png)

- Click it to open the list, then click a symbol to chart it. The current symbol is highlighted.
- With market data connected, each row shows the **price** and the **percent change**, fetched when you open the menu.
- If you have more than one list, the names appear as buttons at the top of the menu. Click one to make it the active list.
- **Manage watchlist…** at the bottom opens the Watchlists screen, where you can add, remove and organize symbols and lists.

An empty list tells you how to add the first symbol: use the star, described next.

## The star: adding a symbol to a watchlist

The **star** at the left of the chart header adds the current symbol to a watchlist. It is filled in yellow when the symbol is already on at least one list.

![The star menu](symbols-star.png)

1. Click the star. A menu lists all your watchlists with a count of symbols in each.
2. Tick a list to add the symbol to it, or untick it to remove the symbol. A small message confirms the change, and a symbol can be on several lists at once.
3. To start a new list, type a name in **New watchlist…** and click **Create** (or press **Enter**). The current symbol is added to the new list.

## The index ticker

The scrolling strip to the right of the watchlist button shows the main market indexes: **S&P 500**, **Dow Jones**, **Nasdaq**, **Russell 2000**, **VIX**, **FTSE 100**, **DAX** and **Nikkei 225**. Each shows its level, the change and the percent change, in green when up and red when down.

- The strip scrolls by itself. **Hover** over it to pause it, which makes a figure easier to read.
- Click an index to chart it.
- With market data connected, the values refresh about once a minute. Without a key, the strip shows sample numbers.
- Charting an index requires your FMP plan to include index data. If it does not, the chart shows a message instead of candles.

## Other ways to change symbol

- **Click a symbol anywhere in the app.** The screener results, market movers, congressional trades, watchlists, active trades, news and journal entries all open the symbol on the chart when you click it.
- **Ask Claude.** In the chat panel, say something like *Show me NVDA on the 5-minute chart* and Claude can move the app to that symbol for you while it explains.
- **The symbol-screen icons** in the chart header (News, Quote Details, Analyst Reports, Fundamentals, Option Stats) always show the current symbol, so switching symbol on one of those screens updates them all.

## Tips

- Type the ticker if you know it, since the first result is usually right. Press **Enter** to take it.
- Keep a short watchlist for the stocks you are practising on, and use a second list for ideas to research.
- Hover over the ticker strip to pause it whenever you want to read it.
