The **Holdings** tab is where you record what you own and see what it is worth today.

![The Holdings tab](portfolio-holdings.png)

> If the portfolio is **linked to a paper account** (see *Portfolio*), the shares and cost of stocks and ETFs come from that account's positions and cannot be edited here. Only mutual funds, which a paper account cannot hold, keep typed numbers. Everything else on this page works the same way.

## Recording what you own

For each holding, click the boxes and type:

- **Shares**: how many you own. Fractions are fine.
- **Total paid**: the total money you paid for those shares, including any fees.

Press **Enter** or click away to save. You can also let the **I bought these** button on the *Plan & calculator* tab fill both in for you, and then correct them if your real fills were different. To record a later purchase by hand, add the new shares to **Shares** and the new cost to **Total paid**.

## What the table shows

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Avg cost** | Total paid divided by shares: your average price |
| **Price** | The latest price (or the one you typed) |
| **Value now** | Shares times price |
| **Gain / loss** | Value now minus total paid, in dollars and percent. Green for a gain, red for a loss |
| **Weight vs target** | The holding's share of the portfolio's value, against its target |

The **Total** row adds it all up. Holdings with no shares are shown with dashes.

### Weight against target

The bar shows the holding's **current share** of your portfolio's value, and the **orange mark** shows its **target**. The text underneath gives both and the difference in percentage points, for example *21.1% of 10% +11.1 pts*. A holding that has grown a lot drifts above its target, and one that fell drifts below it.

That drift is what people mean when they talk about **rebalancing**: bringing the mix back to the target. This app shows the drift but never trades. To fix it you can put new money into the under-target holdings (use **Total size** on the plan tab), or sell some of the ones that have grown at your broker, and think about taxes first.

## Prices

Prices refresh about **every minute** while the screen is open. If a price is missing, a box appears where you can type one. Totals leave out a holding that has shares but no price, and a note says which.

Mutual funds are priced **once a day after the close**, so their value and gain only change then.

## Limits to remember

- **It does not know about dividends, splits or fees** unless you put them in the numbers yourself. A dividend that was paid out to you is not added to the value.
- **It is not a brokerage statement.** What you type is what it uses.
- **Prices may be delayed**, depending on your market data plan.
- An **unlinked** portfolio is separate from the paper trading account: positions you open with the order ticket do not appear here, and these holdings do not affect your paper account. A **linked** portfolio follows the account instead.

## Backing up

Your portfolios are part of the database, so **File → Back Up Data…** saves them and **File → Restore From Backup…** brings them back. See *Data, cache and backups*.
