The **Trading Journal** is where you write down **why** you take trades and what you learn from them. The single biggest difference between people who improve and people who repeat the same mistakes is a record they can look back on. Open it with the **open book** icon on the ribbon.

![An entry for a closed trade](journal-entry.png)

> The journal is yours. Entries are saved on your computer, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask Claude to read one.
>
> The trades in the screenshots are made-up examples.

## What a journal is good for

- **Your reasoning, in your own words.** A week later, you can see whether the idea was good even if the trade lost, or lucky even if it won.
- **Real results.** The app fills in the profit, loss and R multiple from your actual paper fills, so the record cannot be flattered.
- **Patterns.** Searching your entries shows which setups work for you, and which feelings go with your worst trades.
- **A conversation with Claude.** Claude can read an entry and give honest feedback.

## The screen

The screen has a **list** on the left, with summary figures and filters, and the **selected entry** on the right.

![The list of entries](journal-list.png)

### Summary figures

At the top of the list, four numbers sum up your **closed** trades:

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Closed trades** | How many journal trades are finished |
| **Win rate** | The percent that made money |
| **Net P/L** | Total realized profit or loss |
| **Avg R** | The average **R multiple**: the result as a multiple of the amount you planned to risk. *+1.88R* means you made 1.88 times your planned risk |

### Finding entries

- **Search journal…** looks in titles, symbols, reasoning, setups, reviews, lessons and tags.
- The **filter chips** narrow the list: **All**, **Ideas**, **Open**, **Closed**, **Skipped**, **Notes** and **By Claude**.

### The list rows

Each row shows the symbol and direction, a **status badge**, the title, the date, and, for finished trades, the result in dollars and R. A small sparkle marks entries written by Claude.

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Idea** | A plan you or Claude wrote that has not been traded |
| **Order working** | Orders are placed but not yet filled |
| **Open** | A position is open |
| **Closed** | The position was closed, so the result is known |
| **Cancelled** | The orders were cancelled before they filled |
| **Skipped** | You decided not to take it |
| **Note** | A general note, not tied to a trade |

## Creating entries

There are four ways entries get created.

1. **From the order ticket.** For orders that open a position, **Record in trading journal** is ticked by default. It saves your plan, your note and a picture of the chart, and links the orders. See *The order ticket*.
2. **From the Position calculator.** **Save to journal** records the plan as an **idea**. See *Position calculator*.
3. **From Claude.** When Claude proposes a trade, it saves the idea here with its reasoning and the levels, marked as Claude's. See *Trade ideas and the ticket*.
4. **By hand.** **New entry** (at the top right) starts a trade entry for the current symbol, and **New note** starts a general note.

## An entry

An entry is saved **automatically** a moment after you stop typing. *Saving…* and *Saved ✓* appear at the top, so there is no Save button.

### The header

- A **title** you can edit, the **status badge**, and a **Claude** badge if Claude wrote it.
- The date it was created, and *suggested by Claude* where it applies.
- Buttons:
  - **Open in ticket** opens the order ticket pre-filled from the plan. It appears for ideas that have not been traded.
  - **Ask Claude to review** sends the entry to Claude for feedback (see below).
  - **Skip** marks an untraded idea as skipped, and **Reopen** reverses it.
  - The **bin** icon deletes the entry after you confirm. It cannot be undone.

![An idea suggested by Claude](journal-idea.png)

### Type, symbol and setup

- **Type** is **Trade** or **Note / lesson**.
- **Symbol** and **Direction** (Long or Short) apply to trades, and **Open chart** jumps to the symbol.
- **Setup** names the pattern, such as *Pullback to 20-day EMA* or *Breakout retest*. Using consistent names lets you see which setups work.
- **Tags** are words separated by commas, for finding entries later.

### Result

Once orders are linked, the **Result** section shows what really happened, calculated from your fills:

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Working, open or closed |
| **Shares** | The number of shares |
| **Avg entry** and **Avg exit** | The average prices you got |
| **Realized P/L** | The profit or loss locked in |
| **R multiple** | The result divided by the planned risk (entry to stop times shares), shown once the trade is closed |

For an open trade it shows the realized result so far, and updates as fills arrive.

### Plan

**Entry**, **Stop**, **Target** and **Shares**, which you can edit. Below them the journal works out your **planned risk**, **reward** and **reward-to-risk**. Writing the plan down before the trade, and comparing it with the result afterwards, is the heart of the exercise.

### Why this trade?

The box for your **thesis**: what you saw, why now, and **what would prove you wrong**. Be specific. Claude writes its reasoning here too, with simple formatting marks such as `**bold**` that show as plain characters in the box.

### Chart when this was recorded

If a picture of the chart was saved with the entry (the ticket and Claude can do this), it appears here. Click it to enlarge it, and click again to shrink it.

### Review

Fill this in after the trade, and ideally after a skipped idea too.

- **How I felt**: tap any of **Calm**, **Confident**, **Patient**, **Anxious**, **FOMO**, **Greedy**, **Fearful**, **Frustrated** and **Impulsive**. Tap again to remove one.
- **Did I follow my plan?**: **Yes**, **Partly** or **No**.
- **What happened** and **Lesson** boxes: what actually happened and what you will do differently.

Over time, look for patterns: do your worst trades share an emotion such as FOMO, or a plan you did not follow?

### Comments

A thread of comments on the entry. **You** can add your own (type and press **Enter** or click **Add**), and **Claude** adds its feedback here when you ask it to review.

## Asking Claude to review

Click **Ask Claude to review**. Claude reads the entry and replies in the chat, then **saves its feedback as a comment on the entry**. It looks at your reasoning, your risk management and what you can learn. Claude is asked to be honest, not flattering.

You can also say *Write this up as a journal entry* or *Show me my entries with FOMO*, and Claude can create, read and comment on entries. It records the trades it suggests, attributed to itself, but it never sends an order. See *Trade ideas and the ticket*.

## A good habit

1. **Before a trade:** write the thesis, the stop and what would prove you wrong.
2. **After it closes:** fill in how you felt, whether you followed the plan, and one lesson.
3. **Once a week:** filter by **Closed**, look at your win rate and average R, and read the lessons.
4. **Search for repeats.** Search for a setup name or an emotion and read the results together.
