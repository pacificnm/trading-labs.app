The **News** screen lists the latest headlines about the stock you are viewing. Open it with the **newspaper** icon in the chart header, the second icon in the row of symbol screens. It follows the current symbol, so switching symbol switches the news.

![The News screen with headlines for one stock](news-screen.png)

> For general market news, or news about all the stocks in your watchlist and positions, use **Market news** on the ribbon. See *Market news*.

## What you see

At the top, a line reads *Latest headlines for AAPL · refreshes every 5 minutes*, with a **Refresh** button on the right. Below it, each story is a row:

- The **headline**, with a small arrow-in-a-box icon that shows it opens outside the app.
- The **publisher** in bold, such as the news site or wire service.
- How **long ago** it was published (*13 min ago*, *3 h ago*, *2 d ago*), followed by the **exact date and time**.
- A short **summary** of the story.
- A **thumbnail picture** on the right, when the publisher supplied one. If a picture cannot be loaded, the space is left out.

Stories are listed **newest first**, and duplicates are removed.

## Opening a story

Click anywhere on a row to read the full article. It opens in your **web browser**, not inside Trading Lab. Only normal web addresses (`http` and `https`) are opened.

## Times

The date and time on each story are shown in the **time zone you chose** in **Settings**, the same one the chart uses. The app works out which zone the news provider uses, so the times line up with your clock. See *Time zone and display*.

## Keeping up to date

- The screen refreshes itself **every 5 minutes** while you are on it.
- Click **Refresh** to fetch the latest right away. The icon spins while it loads.
- To see older stories, click **Load more** at the bottom. Each click adds 20 more. The button disappears when there is nothing further back.

## When there is nothing to show

- *No recent news for SYMBOL. Index symbols and some ETFs have no company news.* Indexes such as the S&P 500, and many funds, do not have company news. Try a stock, or use **Market news**.
- **An error message with Retry** appears if the news could not be loaded, for example when you are offline.
- **Without an FMP key**, the screen asks you to add one in **File → Settings**. News comes from your market data provider. See *First-time setup*.

## Reading news as a trader

- **Check the time.** A story that is hours old may already be reflected in the price. Compare its time with the moves on the chart. See *The chart screen*.
- **Look for the catalyst.** When a stock makes a sharp move, the news often says why: an earnings report, an upgrade, a product launch or a legal ruling.
- **Be careful with headlines.** A headline is written to be clicked. Read the article before drawing a conclusion, and check whether more than one source reports it.
- **Know what moves the price.** Surprises move prices more than expected news. A good result that analysts expected may do little. See *Analyst reports*.
- **Make a note.** If news is part of your reason for a trade, write it in the journal so you can judge later whether it was a good reason. See *Trading journal*.

## Asking Claude

Claude can read the same headlines. Ask *What is the news on AAPL today and might it explain the move?* or *Summarize the last day of news and tell me what I should check on the chart*. Claude treats the text of articles as information to report, never as instructions, and it will remind you that news is only one input to a decision.
